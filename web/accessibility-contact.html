Accessibility Statement

ORVIKA aims to make its digital safety tools accessible to as many people as possible.

Last reviewed: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the ORVIKA web application

Accessibility commitment

ORVIKA uses WCAG 2.2 Level AA as its web accessibility design and testing target. Implemented work includes keyboard navigation and visible focus, semantic labels and headings, text scaling, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and contrast-aware themes.

The project owner reports completing manual validation of the A11Y-01 to A11Y-10 implementation set. This is a project validation record, not an independent third-party audit or a certification.

Conformance status and limitations

ORVIKA does not claim full WCAG 2.2 AA conformance. Automated tests and project-owner validation do not establish conformance across every page, state, browser, assistive technology, and user configuration. Some third-party and platform behaviours require separate evaluation.

ORVIKA is an evolving application. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please tell us what you were trying to do, the page or feature involved, your browser or assistive technology (if you are comfortable sharing), and the steps needed to reproduce it. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or other sensitive information.

Platform coverage

This statement covers the hosted web experience. The Android application requires separate native evaluation, including TalkBack, Switch Access, and platform display scaling. Web results should not be treated as evidence of Android conformance.
